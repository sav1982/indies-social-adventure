# Lesson 1 — Saying Hello (development)

This branch is separate from `main` so the existing live app stays untouched.

## Learning flow
1. Taylor says 'Hi Indie!' in the approved five-second Runway animation.
2. Pause and let the learner select a short spoken hello, a small wave, or remaining quiet.
3. Show a respectful, realistic outcome for the selected choice; do not treat nervousness or avoiding eye contact as failure.
4. Explore possible feelings and award a Friendship Explorer badge for practising.
