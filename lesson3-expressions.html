Faces and Feelings 🎭

People's faces can give us clues about how they might feel. But a face is not a mind-reading machine!

Let's explore smiles, eyebrows, eyes and body language. There can be more than one explanation.

1. Meet the expressions

Tap each picture. Notice the face, then read what it might mean. Can you see the clues?

Choose a face to explore its clues.

3. Have a go yourself 🪞

Choose an expression to try in a mirror—or simply imagine making it. You do not need to copy it perfectly.

Start with a relaxed face. Notice what your eyebrows and mouth are doing.

It's okay to skip this activity. No camera, photos, or facial scoring are used.
